A short list, kept short on purpose

Five places worth crossing the world for

No ranked countdowns, no thousand-item bucket lists — just five landscapes that earn the trip, and the season, view, and reason to go for each.

01 Santorini, Greece 02 Banff, Canada 03 Kyoto, Japan 04 Machu Picchu, Peru 05 Maldives
01 — The Aegean

Santorini

Cyclades, Greece

Whitewashed villages sit along the rim of a volcanic caldera, looking out over water so blue it barely seems real. Oia's sunset is famous for a reason — the whole town turns gold, then rose, then quiet.

Best timeLate Sept
Known forCaldera views
Do thisWalk Fira→Oia
02 — The Rockies

Banff National Park

Alberta, Canada

Glacier-fed lakes hold a turquoise color that comes from ground-up rock suspended in the water — geology doing the work of a painter. Moraine Lake and Lake Louise are the postcards, but the quieter trails are where the park opens up.

Best timeJun – Sept
Known forGlacial lakes
Do thisHike Plain of Six
03 — Old Japan

Kyoto

Kansai, Japan

Over a thousand temples and shrines are folded into a modern city, so a quiet stone path can lead straight into centuries of history. Come for the vermillion torii gates of Fushimi Inari, stay for the maple trees in autumn.

Best timeNov
Known forTemples, gardens
Do thisFushimi Inari
04 — The Andes

Machu Picchu

Cusco Region, Peru

An Incan citadel built on a mountain ridge, wrapped in cloud forest and terraced fields that still hold their shape 500 years on. Arriving on foot via the Inca Trail changes how the first sight of it lands.

Best timeMay – Sept
Known forIncan ruins
Do thisHike the trail
05 — The Indian Ocean

Maldives

South Asia

A scatter of low coral islands where the water does most of the talking — a visible gradient from pale shallows to deep blue, often crossed with nothing but a stretch of reef. Overwater villas put the ocean directly under the floorboards.

Best timeNov – Apr
Known forReefs, lagoons
Do thisNight snorkel
